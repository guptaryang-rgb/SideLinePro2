Overtaker

Overtaker uses your phone's rear camera and GPS to spot cars you overtake (and cars that overtake you) while you drive — then turns it into a little game with a live tally, a net score, and a trip history you can look back on.

⚠️
Before you start

Mount your phone securely (windshield or dash mount) before driving. Never hold or interact with your phone while the car is moving — only start/end a drive or check your stats when safely parked. Overtaker is for fun and is not a certified speedometer or legal record. Always obey traffic laws and drive attentively.
